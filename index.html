Scan Nest Reader

Scan documents, read PDFs and edit Word files: free, offline and private.

Scan Nest Reader turns your phone into a document scanner, a PDF reader and a Word editor. Everything runs on your device: no account needed, and your documents never leave your device. Free, supported by ads.

  • Scan documents and ID cards with automatic edge detection and filters
  • Read, annotate, sign and fill PDF files; record lectures alongside them
  • Open and edit Word (.docx and .doc) files with familiar Home, Insert, Layout and View tools
  • Recognize text in Arabic and many other languages, scan QR codes, translate on the device
